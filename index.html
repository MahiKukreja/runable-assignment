Video · 1:10Captions burned in
  1. 01

    What works on X right now

    The AI posts that travel are short films with a story, not feature demos. They hook in the first two seconds, carry burned-in captions for people scrolling with sound off, and end on a twist people want to quote-post. Polished AI cinema stops the scroll; an opinion gets the replies.

  2. 02

    Why I made these choices

    It's a satire of vibe-coding that everyone in AI recognises: the 3 a.m. dream of a startup that “prints money”, an AI that says yes to everything, then a public database. The viewer feels smarter than the guy, and Runable gets to make its point: AI does the work, you keep the judgment.

  3. 03

    How I'd measure it

    Views are the vanity number. I'd watch 3-second hold and completion rate, quote-posts and replies (people sharing their own horror stories), bookmarks, and profile visits and clicks to Runable in the first 48 hours, against the median of Runable's recent posts.

02 Part 2 · Motion graphics launch video

Introducing Flow

The brief
Feature
Flow: voice dictation in Runable, like Wispr Flow
Format
16:9 launch film, 29 seconds, music and sound design, no voice-over
Shown in
Notetaker, with fillers and a Tuesday → Thursday slip fixing themselves
Tagline

Speak. Flow. Save.

  1. 01

    The idea: one breath

    Flow turns talking into clean writing, so the film is one continuous move. A voice becomes a line, the line bends into words, and the words land in Notetaker already tidied up. Every shot hands off to the next on a matched motion, so it never feels like a cut.

  2. 02

    Built in Runable's own language

    Cream paper, ink and teal, frosted glass, the Runable sidebar and the real Notetaker page. The film should feel like it came from the product, so nothing in it is generic stock motion, and the logo only appears once, at the end.

  3. 03

    Show the feature, don't say it

    No voice-over and no feature list. You watch the fix happen: “um”s disappear and Tuesday corrects itself to Thursday. I storyboarded it over six rounds of notes before rendering a single frame, then cut it to sound.
